Things I built

Drop-shipping automation for Coupang and Naver Smart Store

Problem
Small sellers lose their time after listing, not before: fulfilling orders by hand, and losing seller score when the source shop runs out of stock.
What I built
A macOS desktop app that takes products picked on Naver, rewrites listings with AI to fit Coupang's rules, publishes to both marketplaces, and runs a pre-publish check. Electron UI, Node daemon, worker processes, Redis queue, plus a Chrome extension.
Outcome
A trial run on the client's own MacBook published 3 real listings to Coupang (Sept 2026). Not yet in paid production. Humans press every button that spends money or publishes.

Unattended digital-product pipeline

Problem
Selling digital products means repeating research, build, English review, store listing, promo copy, and QA for every item. Solo, that caps out at one or two a day.
What I built
An AI agent loop on a home Linux box that wakes up every 2 hours and runs the whole cycle: research, creation, review, Gumroad listing, promo copy, automated QA, and a weekly KPI roll-up.
Outcome
15 products publicly listed on Gumroad. The only human touch is reading the weekly KPI.

Two-room PWA with web push, private network only

Problem
I wanted a household ledger and my agents' status on my phone without two native apps, app-store review, or a public port.
What I built
An installable PWA with a ledger room and an agent room, web push for notifications, reachable only inside a Tailscale private network.
Outcome
In daily use. Ships without an app store.
